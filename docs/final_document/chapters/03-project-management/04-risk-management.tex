\section{Risk Management}
\label{sec:risks}

Every risk in Table~\ref{tab:risks} was observed during the project
rather than anticipated, which is why each has a date and a measured
symptom. Probability and impact are rated low, medium or high as they
stood when the risk appeared; the last column says what was done and
what remains.

Search engines refuse automated queries & High & High & On
25~September, 68 of 69 SearXNG queries came back empty (engines
rate-limited, behind a CAPTCHA or timing out), and every affected claim
looked like an honest \texttt{UNVERIFIED}. Mitigated by a closed engine
list measured one by one, fewer concurrent searches, repeated queries
sent once, a \texttt{searchUnavailable} state reported apart from the
verdicts, and a keyless second route; the health check that sent a
real search to every engine every five seconds was replaced. Remains: a
search from a data-centre address is untested, and an API-keyed engine
is future work (Section~\ref{sec:future-work}). \\
\addlinespace
The language model times out on a CPU & High & High & On 1~October
the first analysis on the production stack, CPU only, turned 3 of 4
verdicts into ``provider unreachable'' under timeouts sized for a GPU.
Mitigated by separate production limits (one call at a time, 180~s)
and by reporting \texttt{llmUnreachable} apart from verdicts. Remains:
the development defaults are still sized for a GPU. \\
\addlinespace
Sources block the scraper & Medium & Medium & EFE (HTTP 403) and
Reuters (401) refused every request from 23~September to 6~October and
were disabled on 6~October; they remain rated as evidence. Feeds that
died (National Geographic, SINC, CNN, WHO) are read through section
pages, a news sitemap or an API instead. The scraper names itself and
does not try to pass for a browser. \\
\addlinespace
Labelling takes longer than planned & High & High & Found on
28~September (the set is a 40-hour task, rebalanced). By 14~October an
AI labelling pass had labelled 78 of 109 natural facts to keep pace,
which created a second risk: a gold set labelled by a model, used to
judge a model. Mitigated by disclosing every AI label in its record and
in this document, by making the annotator's review a condition of the
set, and by measuring the agreement between the two as a result (RQ3).
Remains: the review itself. \\
\addlinespace
No refuted claims in the domain & High & High & None of 109 natural
claims was \texttt{FALSE}, so an always-\texttt{TRUE} system would
score 83\%. Mitigated by 30 synthetic refuted claims built by
substitution, as FEVER and SciFact built theirs, and reported apart
(Subsection~\ref{subsec:custom-mutations}). \\
\addlinespace
The cloud deployment is never applied & Medium & Low & The
infrastructure is written and checked against a local emulator, which
runs no guest, so the virtual machine's boot is unverified. The thesis
does not depend on it: every result is produced on the development
stack. Left as future work, with a step-by-step runbook. \\
\addlinespace
Scope grows faster than results & Medium & High & Features added
outside the plan in early October (Section~\ref{sec:deviations}) were
each useful and measured, while the research questions had no answer.
Mitigated by the feature freeze and the priorities of 14~October. \\
\addlinespace
Native crash in the evaluation harness & Low & Low & The harness
process crashed twice in native code while parsing scraped pages
during the Spanish X-Fact run. Absorbed by the resume rule, which
re-runs exactly the claims a crash lost; the cause is not located. \\
