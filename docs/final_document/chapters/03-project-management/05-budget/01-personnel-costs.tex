\subsection{Personnel Costs}
\label{subsec:personnel-costs}

The 900 hours of Table~\ref{tab:phases} are carried by one person, but
they are costed as the four roles a team would need. Each phase is
assigned to the role whose work it is: building the system to the
developer (Phases~0, 2, 3 and~6, and Sprint~1 of Phase~1); the
validation set, the harness, the experiments and the model benchmarks
to the data scientist (Phases~4 and~5); Sprint~2 and the transversal
testing track to the tester; and planning, replanning and the report to
the project manager (Phase~7). The allocation is approximate --- the
foundations also contain modelling work, and every phase some planning
--- but it adds up to the plan.
