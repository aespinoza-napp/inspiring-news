\subsection{Contingency and Total}

Two unforeseen costs are identified and priced in advance: one month of
the cloud virtual machine for a live demonstration (about 115~USD), and
the hosted verifier model of the model comparison, whose price is read
on the day of the run. The rest of the risk in this project is in hours
rather than money (Section~\ref{sec:risks}), and is absorbed by the
replanning rule of Section~\ref{subsec:replanning}: a task that grows is
paid for by shrinking another. A contingency margin of 10\% is applied
to the personnel and hardware costs to cover what the plan does not
foresee.

\pending{the total: personnel (Table~\ref{tab:personnel-hours} times
the hourly costs), amortised hardware, software (zero), infrastructure
(zero to date, 115~USD with the demonstration month), the hosted model
run, and the 10\% contingency.}
