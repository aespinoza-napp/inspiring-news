\subsection{Cost and Latency}

For the baseline on the X-Fact pilot, the harness recorded 63 model
calls (one claim had no evidence and was not sent to the model), none
failed, with 2,141 prompt and 179 completion tokens per claim at the
median, 28.3 completion tokens per second, and a model call of 5.6~s at
the median (90th percentile 9.5~s, maximum 14.5~s) on the development
laptop. Its per-token cost is zero; its cost is the machine's time.
