\subsection{Cost and Response Time}
\label{subsec:results-response-time}

Every model here is local and openly licensed: its per-token cost is
zero, and its cost is the machine's time. Response time is therefore
reported beside quality for every role, measured on the development
machine's processor (no graphics card), each model alone on it: two
runs at once share the processor, and their times are each other's
waiting (Table~\ref{tab:response-time}).

The model is three quarters of a claim's time (77\% for the baseline),
and about a third of its span is waiting for the other claim's call:
retrieval and ranking together take 14 seconds. A faster verifier is
therefore the only large speed-up left, and the candidate tried was
slower as well as less accurate. End to end, an article of four claims
took 184 seconds in the production stack on 1~October; a draft takes
about three minutes and its review about three and a half more, with
one model swap between them, since the production stack holds one model
in memory at a time (Section~\ref{sec:dev-deployment}).

For the X-Fact pilot of 13~October, on the development laptop's
graphics card, the baseline's model call took 5.6~s at the median
(90th percentile 9.5~s), 28.3 completion tokens per second against 5.2
on the processor: the same model is about six times faster on a
graphics card, which is the hardware choice the deployment's cost
estimate does not yet include.
