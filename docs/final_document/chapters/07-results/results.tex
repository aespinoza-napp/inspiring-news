\chapter{Results}
\label{ch:results}

This chapter reports what has been measured as of 16~October~2026,
question by question, and says plainly where a measurement has not been
made yet. Two kinds of result are distinguished throughout: those
measured against a person's judgement, and those measured against
labels assigned by the AI labelling pass and not yet reviewed by the
annotator, which are indications until that review is done
(Section~\ref{subsec:custom-procedure}).
