\subsection{General Objective}

To design, build and evaluate a low-cost, end-to-end system that finds
constructive news on topics chosen by an editor, in English and
Spanish, and verifies its claims against evidence retrieved from the
open web, measuring at each stage how close it comes to the work of a
journalist.
