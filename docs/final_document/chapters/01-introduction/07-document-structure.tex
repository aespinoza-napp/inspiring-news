\section{Structure of the Document}
\label{sec:structure}

Chapter~\ref{ch:state-of-the-art} reviews automated fact-checking, its
benchmarks, constructive journalism, news selection and the existing
tools, and positions this work among them.
Chapter~\ref{ch:project-management} describes how the project was
planned and managed, how the plan changed, its risks and its budget.
Chapter~\ref{ch:methodology} specifies the system: its requirements,
architecture, data model and the design of each stage.
Chapter~\ref{ch:development} describes how it was built and tested.
Chapter~\ref{ch:evaluation} fixes how each research question is
measured and describes the datasets and the evaluation harness.
Chapter~\ref{ch:results} reports what was measured and discusses it.
Chapter~\ref{ch:sustainability} analyses the project's sustainability
and its ethical implications, and Chapter~\ref{ch:conclusions} answers
the research questions and lists the work left. The appendices give
the detailed planning, the source catalogue, the annotation guide and
the commands that reproduce every result.
