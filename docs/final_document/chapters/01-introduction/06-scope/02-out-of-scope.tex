\subsection{Out of Scope}

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{User accounts, profiles, bookmarks and
    recommendations}, cut on 29~September~2026: none of them answers a
    research question, and each is a subsystem to build, secure and
    test. The reader view is what remains of the user-facing
    application.
    \item \textbf{The recommendation system and the mobile
    application} of the wider project (docs/arquitectura-tecnica.md,
    sections~3 and~4). The graph database this work writes is their
    foundation, but neither is built.
    \item \textbf{Evaluating the writing flow}, and publishing to
    social networks. The flow is built (Section~\ref{sec:dev-writing}),
    but none of the four research questions asks for it, so its
    measurement --- the share of supported claims and the precision of
    its citations --- is future work (Section~\ref{sec:future-work}),
    as are the media a social network would need.
    \item \textbf{Training or fine-tuning any model.} Every model is
    used as published; nothing is trained on the evaluation data.
    \item \textbf{Languages other than English and Spanish.} A source
    in a third language is refused by the test suite rather than scored
    with the English word lists.
    \item \textbf{Paid search or model APIs}, and a real cloud
    deployment: the infrastructure is written and checked against an
    emulator, but has never been applied to a billed project.
\end{itemize}
