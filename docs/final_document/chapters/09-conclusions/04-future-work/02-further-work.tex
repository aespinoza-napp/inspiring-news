\subsection{Further Work}
\label{subsec:future-further}

\paragraph{Writing constructive news from verified sources.} The same
story from two or three outlets --- which the ranking already groups ---
turned into a short constructive piece that cites its sources; its
claims extracted by the pipeline's own extractor and each verified
against the given sources only, with no web search; scored by the
share of supported claims \cite{min2023factscore} and the precision and
recall of its citations \cite{gao2023alce}, validated by a hand check.
The pipeline's verifier would become the evaluator of its own writer.
It was moved out of scope on 14~October because no research question
asks it.

\paragraph{Evidence against the age of a claim.} Re-running retrieval on
the same recent claims after a week and after a month, to put a number
on how much evidence a claim gains as it ages --- the question that
benchmarks of fresh knowledge ask of language models \cite{vu2024freshllms}
and that the custom set's Rule~1 meets every day, when a new study has
only its own press release.

\paragraph{The rest of the product.} The recommendation system over the
graph, with the verdict as one of its signals, and the mobile
application of the wider project (Section~\ref{sec:sota-selection}).

\paragraph{Operations.} The first real cloud deployment, with a search
check from a data-centre address; reading \texttt{robots.txt}; evicting
finished jobs; expiring the analysis cache; normalising URLs before
comparing them; and the graph measured at scale beyond a few thousand
articles.

\paragraph{Quality.} End-to-end tests of the frontend; a policy for the
three bluffs that still pass the guardrails; separating ``how much
could be checked'' from ``what was found'' in the article's verdict;
and the topic classifier, measured on the reviewed set and improved if
it matters.
