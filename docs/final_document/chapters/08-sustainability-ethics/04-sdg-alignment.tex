\section{Alignment with the Sustainable Development Goals}
\label{sec:sdg}

The project relates most directly to one goal, and indirectly to two.

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{SDG~16, Peace, justice and strong institutions},
    target~16.10: ensure public access to information. Verified,
    inspectable news on topics people can act on is a contribution to
    the quality of the information the public has access to; a system
    that reports what it could not verify, and why, contributes to it
    more honestly than one that does not.
    \item \textbf{SDG~9, Industry, innovation and infrastructure}: a
    verification pipeline that runs on open models and free software, on
    one modest machine, lowers the cost of a capability that is otherwise
    available only to large organisations.
    \item \textbf{The goals of the topics themselves} --- health (SDG~3),
    climate (SDG~13), education (SDG~4) --- are served only indirectly,
    by making constructive reporting on them easier to find and to check.
\end{itemize}

The contribution is modest and indirect: the system is a research
prototype, its verifier is not yet accurate enough to be relied on, and
its effect on the public's access to information has not been measured.
