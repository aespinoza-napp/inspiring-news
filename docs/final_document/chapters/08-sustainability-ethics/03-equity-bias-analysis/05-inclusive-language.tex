\subsection{Inclusive Language, References and Examples}

This document refers to people by role --- the annotator, the editor,
the reader, the journalist --- and uses gender-neutral forms when a
person's gender is not known or not relevant (``they'', ``themself'').
Examples are taken from the system's real data, and were chosen to
illustrate a mechanism, not a group of people; claims attributed to
named people are discussed as claims, without judgement of the person.
The figures and tables do not rely on colour alone to carry meaning.
The defence will follow the same conventions.

The cited literature reflects the field as it is, which is largely
English-language and from European and North American institutions;
the system's own limitations in the same direction are stated in
Section~\ref{sec:bias}.
