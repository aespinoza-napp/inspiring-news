\subsection{Functional Requirements}

\begin{table}[ht]
\centering
\small
\caption{Functional requirements.}
\label{tab:functional-requirements}
\begin{tabularx}{\linewidth}{@{}lX@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{Id} & \textbf{The system shall\ldots} \\
\midrule
FR1 & \textbf{Discover}: read the configured sources for up to three
topic groups and list their recent articles with title, summary,
date and language. \\
FR2 & \textbf{Screen and rank}: leave out what the publication never
covers and what is not an article, and order the rest by how well it
fits the mission and the topics asked for. \\
FR3 & \textbf{Select}: let a person, a language model, or both choose
at most twenty candidates to analyse, and record who chose what. \\
FR4 & \textbf{Extract}: turn any article URL into its text, title,
author and publication time. \\
FR5 & \textbf{Enrich}: annotate each article with topics, entities,
keywords, sentiment, editorial quality, candidate claims and an
embedding. \\
FR6 & \textbf{Admit}: decide whether an article is on topic,
constructive enough and not a duplicate before any evidence is sought,
and record every reason it is turned away. \\
FR7 & \textbf{Verify}: for each selected claim, retrieve evidence from
the open web and the system's own corpus, rank it, and return one of
five verdicts with a confidence, an explanation and the evidence it
cites. \\
FR8 & \textbf{Report failures as failures}: mark a claim whose search
or model could not be reached as such, apart from the verdicts. \\
FR9 & \textbf{Store}: keep the raw, processed and served form of every
analysis with its lineage, and write it to a graph database. \\
FR10 & \textbf{Publish}: show readers the published articles with each
claim's verdict, its evidence and why each source was trusted. \\
FR11 & \textbf{Trace}: show, during and after a run, every query sent,
every source found and the rating each received. \\
FR12 & \textbf{Evaluate}: run the claim-checking stage over a labelled
set and report its metrics with their uncertainty. \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}
\end{table}
