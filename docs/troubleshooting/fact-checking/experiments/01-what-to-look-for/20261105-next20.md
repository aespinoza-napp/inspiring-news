## Findings

cause, so the patterns below are listed by claim, not summed.

1. **The six term slots go to the sentence's framing, in sentence order, and the finding
   comes after them** (stage 01, `checkable_point`: `terms[:MAX_TERMS]`, `MAX_TERMS = 6`,
   over `content_terms`, which keeps the order words appear). Who did or said it comes
   first in a news sentence; what was found comes last and is cut.

     glucocorticoides; `missing`: disfunción diastólica.
   - fact086: `terms` = primera, autora, investigadora, resultados, muestran, obesidad;
     `missing`: hiperglucemia, remodelación molecular, cardiomiocitos, macrófagos.

     `missing`: cartilage, older mice, knee.

## Proposed change

**What.** Order the checkable point's terms so that the sentence's framing (who did, said
or published it) goes last instead of first. The words are still all there, only
demoted, so a short sentence keeps them and a claim *about* its author can still
reach them past the finding's words.

**Where.** `backend/src/services/fact_checker/retrieval/checkable.py`, `checkable_point`:
before `terms[:MAX_TERMS]`, a stable sort that moves words in a new `FRAMING` lexicon
(both languages, invariant 8; Spanish entries as `*` prefixes, like `lexicons.py`) after
the others, each group in sentence order. The lexicon is word classes, not this batch's
words:
- reporting and attribution: said, adds, reveals, according; dijo, añadió, según
  (those in `FILLERS` stay there);
- research roles and acts: study, led, author, researcher, colleague, professor,
  associate, results, showed, discovered, identified, published; estudio, liderado,
  dirigido, autor*, investigador*, profesor*, resultados, muestran, identificado,
  publicado, participaron;
- discourse framing: later, main, goal.

**Lever.** A new per-run field in `src/config/thresholds.py` (resolved class and
overrides, invariants 1-2): `checkable_terms: "sentence" | "framing_last"`, default
`"sentence"`, so every earlier run and this batch's before stay reproducible. Threaded
through `query_builder.plan_queries` / `plan_checkable` and `troubleshoot.plan_for`, or
the offline measure cannot see it. Only read under `query_plan: checkable`.

**Mode.** Offline for this stage (the checkable point, no search). Searched for verdicts:
it changes the queries.

**Prediction** (from reading the code, then corrected by the implementation's tests,
which run the batch's sentences; the script measures it):
- fact086 gains hiperglucemia and remodelación molecular (0.333 -> 0.667); fact088 gains
  older mice (0.5 -> 0.667); fact122 gains third (0.5 -> 0.667, "main goal" moved out).
  Cartilage and knee (fact088), cardiomiocitos and macrófagos (fact086) stay cut: they
  are past six even then.
- fact085 **unchanged** (0.75), against this section's first reading: with "estudio
  liderado identificado" moved last, "mecanismo causal", which is not framing, still
  takes slots five and six, so "disfunción" gets in and "diastólica" stays seventh;
  the detail counts as missing. `checkablePointAllCarried` stays 5.
- fact106, fact123, fact125 and fact099 unchanged: their framing is not of these
  classes ("later" aside, and fact106's lost words are further still), or the lost word
  is seventh or later regardless (fact125's "hormonales").
- `checkablePointCoverage` 0.732 -> about 0.765 (three claims up by a third or a sixth);
  `sentencePlanCoverage` unchanged (0.388): the change is read under `query_plan:
  checkable` only.
- fact088's proposition query takes `Blau orthopedic surgery older mice` in place of
  `Blau colleague associate professor orthopedic`.

**Built** (`--implement`): `checkable.FRAMING` and `framing_last`, `checkable_point(...,
terms_order)`, threaded through `plan_checkable` / `plan_queries` /
`SearchProvider.plan`, the retriever's `searching_web` record and `troubleshoot.plan_for`
/ `offline_plans`; `PipelineThresholds.checkable_terms` and its override, default
`"sentence"` (`settings.CHECKABLE_TERMS`), out of a run's key at that value
(`runner._SPEED_BEFORE`), search-side for the loop (`iterate.SEARCH_SIDE`). Switched on
by `{"query_plan": "checkable", "checkable_terms": "framing_last"}`.

**What could get worse.** A claim whose point *is* its framing ("first author", "professor
of", a study's lead) loses those words from `terms[:5]`. The lexicon is written by looking at
train claims, so it can fit them: before it is adopted, measure it offline on all 69 train
claims and on the 4 Nov batch's 20 (`experiments/claude-search/plan.jsonl`), and no claim's share
may fall.
Not fixed by it, and next in line: figures joined to letters (finding 2, fact099 and fact101,
where the figure is the claim) and the possessive and cap losses in names (finding 3).
