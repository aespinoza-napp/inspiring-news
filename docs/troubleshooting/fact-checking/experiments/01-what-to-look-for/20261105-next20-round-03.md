## Proposed change

**What.** A third order for the checkable point's terms, `finding_first`:
the finding's words, then the sentence's *asides*, then its framing, each
group in sentence order. Asides are the closed classes the stopword lists
leave out (prepositions, adverbs of place, time and degree, modals:
`checkable.ASIDES`) and a verb in the simple past (`checkable.PAST_TENSE`:
English `-ed` but not `-eed` or four letters, so not "need", "speed",
"used"; Spanish `-ó`, `-aron`, `-ieron`, read on the accented word, so
"llenó" but not "estado"). Not `-ing` ("monitoring" is the camera), not
"bajo" ("más bajo" is what fact124 claims). Moved, never dropped: under six
terms the same words come back. It is `framing_last` with the asides
moved too, so it replaces the base's `framing_last`.

**Where.** `retrieval/checkable.py`: `ASIDES`, `PAST_TENSE`,
`finding_first()`, called from `checkable_point()` when `terms_order` is
"finding_first". The value is added to the `checkable_terms` Literal in
`config/thresholds.py` (resolved and overrides) and `CHECKABLE_TERMS` in
`config/settings.py`. Default `sentence` unchanged, and `framing_last` is
as it was. No new plumbing: `point_for_thresholds` and
`query_builder.plan_arguments` already pass `checkable_terms`.

**Lever.** `checkable_terms: finding_first`. Offline for this stage; the
order also changes the proposition (`terms[:5]`) and specifics
(`terms[:3]`) queries, so its effect on verdicts is a searched try.

**Prediction** (by hand from the code and the terms in
`comparison-before.json`, since running Python on the batch needed an
approval this session did not have; the figures are the script's):
- fact123 gains "monitoring cameras" ("above", "snapped" move behind
  "cameras"): share 0.8 -> 1.0, so `checkablePointAllCarried` 7 -> 8.
- fact088 gains "cartilage" ("reversed" moves; "older mice" stays): 0.667
  -> 0.833. "knee" stays missing (twelfth word).
- fact106 unchanged: "arrived" and "back" move, but "maple" and "crew" are
  still past the sixth. fact085, fact086 unchanged (finding 1).
- No other claim's carried details change: fact090 keeps summer and
  sewage, fact101 keeps prices and therm, fact122 keeps "third", fact117
  keeps "llenó" (moved after "primera inicios", three terms only).
- `checkablePointCoverage` rises from 0.832 by about 0.018 (two claims'
  shares; the script computes the figure). `sentencePlanCoverage` stays
  0.523 (the sentence plan is untouched).

**What could get worse.** (1) A past-tense verb that *is* the finding
("banned", "approved", "infected") goes behind the other content words;
in a sentence with more than six it is cut. The likeliest loss on the
held-out batch. (2) An `-ed` noun or adjective ("hundred", "sacred") is
moved the same way. (3) A preposition in a name the entity model missed
is already not a name, so nothing new. (4) Queries change for claims that
lose no detail here (fact090, fact099, fact100, fact101, fact122): only a
searched try says whether the engines care.
