## Findings

Read from `round-04/comparison-before.json`, which is the stage under the base (`finding_first`, `with_units`). Earlier rounds fixed framing, asides and unit figures. What is left falls into four groups.

1. **The six term slots run out before the finding's last words (4 claims, stage 01).** In each case the missing detail is a word the sentence holds, but it comes after six other terms. Cutting at `MAX_TERMS` drops it, so nothing was lost by misreading.
   - fact085: `counted.checkablePoint.missing` is "disfunción diastólica". `pipeline.checkablePoint.terms` ends at "disfunción"; "activación", "mecanismo" and "causal" took earlier slots.
   - fact086: "macrófagos" is missing. "primera" and "provocan" took slots, and the enumeration after the colon runs past the cut.
   - fact088: "knee" is missing. "orthopedic surgery" (Bhutani's role) and "blocking" took slots.
   - fact106: "maple" and "crew" are missing. The terms are "debris laden front lawn minutes lumber": "minutes" is the noun of the figure "30", but it takes a slot as a word of its own. That is the one slot "crew" needed.

   Of these, only the counted noun is a class of word that can be bound elsewhere without a lexicon.
2. **The subject or detail is not in the sentence (4 claims, stage 01, not fixable from the sentence).**
   - fact107: "Wyandotte County" and "community has come together" (the point's `subject` comes from the headline: Strangers, House, Tarp).
   - fact114: "2050", the claim's date plus "próximos 24 años".
   - fact117: "Movistar Arena", the venue's current name.
   - fact118: "ONT", the body behind the statistic.

   These need world knowledge or a neighbouring sentence: `claim_restatement`, or the article's other sentences.
3. **Names are broken or cut (3 claims, stage 01).**
   - fact089: "A Guide to Mudlarking on the River Thames" is read as "A Guide" + "Mudlarking", because "to" and "on" do not join a name.
   - fact122: "3I/ATLAS" is read as "Comet" + "ATLAS", because a word with a digit breaks a name, and "Juice’s" takes a fourth name slot beside "Juice". "lunar-Earth" falls past the term cut.
   - fact124: "Universidad de Barcelona" is past `MAX_NAMES` = 4, because the names come in entity-label order, not sentence order.
4. **A three-letter noun is never a term (1 claim, stage 01).** fact099 is missing "gas imports": `terms.WORD` takes four letters or more, so "gas" cannot be a term. This is one claim, so it is not a pattern yet.

The pipeline names no `sourceType` for any claim (`pipelineNamesSourceType`: false in all 20). Claude named one for all 20 (`claudeNamedSourceType`: 20).

Worth a second look at the label: none in this stage. No claim here turns on a gold/Claude disagreement.

What this batch does not show: 20 claims and 104 details, so each claim's details move coverage by several hundredths. The decisive details are Claude's own judgement. Coverage of the point is not coverage of the queries: the proposition query takes `terms[:5]` and the specifics query `terms[:3]`, so the sixth term reaches no query. The query side is stage 02's.

## Proposed change

**What:** a new value of the existing threshold, `checkable_figures: counted`. It reads figures as `with_units` does, and also keeps the noun a figure counts beside it when the sentence writes one right after the number: "30 minutes", "116 mujeres", "24 años", "41% share". This is the spaced form of the `COUNTED` identifier "11-set", which the module already keeps whole. Because the noun is inside a figure, it leaves the terms (`inside`), and the next finding word takes its slot.

**Where:** `checkable.py`, in the new `counted_figures` function, called from `checkable_point` after the figures are separated from the dates and before `inside` is read. The noun must:
- be lowercase (a capital is a name) and four letters or more;
- not be a stopword, filler or month;
- not be an aside or a simple-past verb ("£5.9bn since" stays "£5.9bn");
- not be the first half of a compound ("212 travel-related" stays "212").

The number and order of figures are unchanged. The `Literal` gains the value in `settings.py` and in both classes in `thresholds.py`. No new plumbing is needed, because `plan_arguments` and `point_for_thresholds` already pass `checkable_figures`.

**Lever:** `{"checkable_figures": "counted"}`, which replaces the base's `with_units` with a superset of it. The defaults `bare` and `with_units` are unchanged. This is an offline measure.

**Prediction:**
- fact106 gains "crew": the terms become "debris laden front lawn lumber crew", and its share goes 0.5 → 0.667. The new test asserts this on the sentence with that run's entity names.
- fact100 ("41% share"), fact125 ("116 mujeres") and fact114 ("24 años") change shape but neither gain nor lose a detail.
- No other tune claim has a non-year figure followed by an eligible noun.
- `checkablePointCoverage` should rise a little from 0.85. `checkablePointAllCarried` and `sentencePlanCoverage` should stay the same. The script computes the figures.
- The rule cannot lower coverage on any batch, the held-out one included: the noun stays in the point, the figure count is unchanged, and the terms can only gain a word.

**What could get worse:** these are search-side effects, measured by a searched try (the `iterate` line in `proposal.json`), not here.
- The anchor query quotes figures, so "30 minutes" becomes a phrase that a page writing "half an hour" misses.
- A figure ending in a word matches `DISTINCTIVE`, so the claim becomes `specific` and gets the specifics query instead of the refutation query. This follows how "11-set" is treated, but "30 minutes" identifies less than "116 mujeres".
- A word that follows a number without being what it counts ("page 12 shows") gets bound to it.
