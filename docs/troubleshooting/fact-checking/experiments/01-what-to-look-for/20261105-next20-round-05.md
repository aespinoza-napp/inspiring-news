## Proposed change

**What.** When the cut to six terms takes the sentence's closing finding word, keep it as a seventh term, and add that word to the proposition query. The closing finding word is the last term in sentence order that is neither an aside nor framing. A sentence tends to put what is new last (end-focus, in both languages), and this rule uses no list of this batch's words. `finding_first`'s six terms stay exactly as they are, and so does a sentence whose closing word already made the cut.

**Where.** `checkable.finding_closing` (new), selected in `checkable_point`; `query_builder.plan_checkable` adds `point.terms[MAX_TERMS:]` to the proposition. The anchor and third queries are untouched.

**Lever.** `checkable_terms: finding_closing`, a new value in `PipelineThresholds`, `ThresholdOverrides` and `settings.CHECKABLE_TERMS`. The default stays `sentence`, and `finding_first` stays reachable and stops at six. Offline for this stage; a searched try for verdicts.

**Why not a bigger cap.** It would need many more slots to reach "knee" or "macrófagos", and without a change to the plan none of those words would be searched (finding 3).

**Prediction.** fact086 carries macrófagos and fact088 carries knee, confirmed on those two sentences by the new tests (with fact086's names written by hand from the record's `names`). Each is one of six details, so coverage should go from 0.858 to about 0.875 (my arithmetic, not a measured figure) and `checkablePointAllCarried` should go from 8 to 10, since that was the only detail missing from each. No claim can lose a detail, because the six are unchanged and only a word is added. This round does not fix fact085, fact106 or fact122, whose words are not the closing ones, nor findings 4-6.

**What could get worse.** The closing word is sometimes not the point: reading the sentences, "the figure below" (fact101) would add "figure", and "Carbon Brief analysis" (fact099) would add "analysis". This measure cannot see that, because a word added to the point can only raise coverage. So the held-out batch not getting worse is weak evidence here, since this rule can hardly make it worse. The real cost is one more word in the proposition query, on sentences with more than six terms, and that query's effect on what the search returns is unmeasured: it needs a searched try. Searched runs differ by 6-9 points on their own, so a small difference there will say nothing.
