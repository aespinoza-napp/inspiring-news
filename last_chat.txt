**Problems found while reading the code and data:**
- **No `language` argument:** `check_claim` doesn't take one, so the 40 Spanish pilot claims would be searched with the English word lists.
- **No `context` argument:** a custom-set claim could be "verified" by the article it came from. G2 fixes both before producing any number.
- **The pilot can find its own answers:** all 40 chequeado.com rows link chequeado's own verdict page and have no claim date. A search of today's web will find those verdicts. The harness reports these claims separately rather than hiding them.
- **The custom set is lopsided:** 27 of its 37 facts are `TRUE` and none is `FALSE`.
