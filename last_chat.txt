You need to rebuild the backend container before using it. Your Docker backend runs the old code, so /discover won't work there until you run docker compose up --build backend. Your frontend dev server on port 3000 already shows the new pages, since it watches the source folder.

1. Scraping by topic.

How it works: you pick 1–3 of the five topic groups (Society, Science, Environment, Culture, Health). Each source file now says which groups it covers, and every group has at least one English and one Spanish source.
What it drops: sport, celebrity, horoscope, lottery and weather sections, before anything is fetched. Spanish feeds are now filtered by Spanish words in the title when a run is scoped to topics.
What it saves: live, Environment read 14 of 34 sources and 10% of the links. El País kept 18 of its 159 items and the election coverage disappeared.
2. Choosing what to analyse. /discover lists the articles found with their title and summary. You tick up to 20, or press Let the AI choose: the LLM scores each 0–10 for positive impact with a one-line reason and ticks its picks, which you can change. Only the ticked articles are analysed. On llama3.1 it handled 26 articles in 167 s; wind and solar savings scored 10, and every election story and the comedy photo awards scored 0. Each round is saved with who chose what.

3. Consistency.

Navigation: two groups, Newsroom (Discover → Analyze → Live → Reader, with the same step bar on each) and Lab (the tool pages).
Ingest panel: the old one on /scraper is gone in favour of Discover.
Shared definitions: the topic groups are defined once in the backend, and the link-safety check is one helper instead of three copies.
4. Experiment tools. uv run python -m src.evaluation.experiments with discovery, selection, blind-ai, admission or topics; docs/experiments.md explains how to run each and what it means. First results:

Topic classifier: it gets the topic group right for 73% of your labelled articles, and leaves 10% with no topic, which admission then rejects.
Admission threshold: raising the topic minimum from 0.35 to 0.40 would admit 61% of those articles instead of 85%.

Choices I made that you may want to change:

Which groups each source belongs to: in each source file, set by my judgement of what it publishes.
The off-topic section list.
The AI's pick threshold of 6/10.

Known weaknesses:
